# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/),
version 3.0.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, keep discussions about the work, and do not
harass anyone. Reports of unacceptable behavior go to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting form (it accepts non-security reports too) or by opening an issue asking for a private channel.
Reports are handled confidentially and may lead to a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the
project's spaces.
